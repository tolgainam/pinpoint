  • 1Current values as chips. Computed styles mapped to the project's tokens. The amber chip is a value that maps to no token — you learn the element is off-system before typing a word.
  • 2The picker is the project's own scale, rendered as a ramp — not a free-form number input. Grey is where the element sits today; terracotta is your pick.

Scene 03 · Selection depth control

Walk the ancestor chain before you pin

Click grabs whatever node is on top — often a span three levels under the thing you meant. After clicking, ↑ and ↓ move the highlight through the ancestors; the breadcrumb shows where you are, and the screenshot re-crops to the final choice.
