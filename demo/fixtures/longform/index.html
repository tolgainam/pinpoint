Route in focus

Four days on the Laugavegur

Day 1 of 4

  1. Day 1 · Landmannalaugar to Hrafntinnusker · 12 km

    Rhyolite, obsidian, and a hot river

    The trail climbs 450 m out of the valley through hills striped orange, green and grey. Soak in the hot river before you leave; there is not another one for four days.

  2. Day 2 · Hrafntinnusker to Álftavatn · 12 km

    Snowfields in July

    The highest night of the trek. The hut sits among steam vents and old snow, and on a clear September night the sky does the rest.

  3. Day 3 · Álftavatn to Emstrur · 15 km

    Two river crossings and a lake

    Bring sandals. The Bláfjallakvísl is knee-deep and glacial, and it is the part everyone talks about at dinner.

  4. Day 4 · Emstrur to Þórsmörk · 16 km

    Down into the birch woods

    Black sand gives way to the Markarfljót canyon, then to Þórsmörk's birches: the first trees in 55 km, and a bus back to Reykjavík.

Before you ask

Questions we answer every week

How fit do I need to be?

If you can walk six hours with a light pack two days running, you can do every route here except Annapurna and Huayhuash, which need a few weeks of hills first.

What is included in the price?

Guides, huts or camps, most meals, luggage transfers and the transfer from the nearest airport. Flights and travel insurance are yours.

Can I come on my own?

About a third of our travellers do. We pair solo travellers in twin rooms at no extra cost, or you can pay a single supplement.

What happens if the weather closes the route?

The guide reroutes, and every route has a planned bad-weather variant. If a whole departure is cancelled, you get a full refund or a free transfer to another date.

When does 2027 booking open?

On 1 November. People on the list below get a 48-hour head start.
