Pinpoint

Comment on any element of your local site and send it to Claude Code.

Get started

  1. 1Pin the icon: puzzle piece > pin Pinpoint
  2. 2Open your dev server, e.g. localhost:5173
  3. 3Click the Pinpoint icon to show the toolbar
  4. 4Click Comment, then click an element

Screenshots

By default Chrome allows a screenshot only after you click the Pinpoint icon, and forgets it each time the page loads. Comments still send without one.

To skip that click, let Pinpoint take screenshots on every page. Your browser words this as "read and change your data on all websites". Pinpoint uses it only to take the picture when you add a comment. You can turn it off here at any time.
